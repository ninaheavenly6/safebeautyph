About the Initiative

This platform aims to raise awareness regarding the importance of hygiene in cosmetic testing environments. It addresses risks associated with unsanitary tester usage and highlights the best practices for both retailers and consumers.

With proper education and sanitation protocols, users can significantly reduce the risk of skin and eye infections caused by contaminated cosmetic testers.

Hygiene Guidelines

Customer Hygiene Guidelines

  • Wash or Sanitize Hands

    Always clean your hands before testing any cosmetic products to prevent contamination.

  • Use Disposable Applicators

    Only use single-use applicators provided by the store. Never use testers directly on your skin.

  • Avoid Broken Skin

    Do not test products on cuts, acne, or irritated skin to prevent infections.

Additional Safety Tips

  • Avoid Eyes and Mouth

    Never test products directly on your eyes or lips where infections can spread easily.

  • Check Expiration Dates

    Always verify the expiration date before using any cosmetic tester.

  • Report Unhygienic Conditions

    Notify store staff immediately if testers appear dirty or contaminated.

Retail Staff Responsibilities

  • Disinfect After Each Use

    Thoroughly clean and disinfect all testers after every customer interaction.

  • Regular Tester Replacement

    Replace cosmetic testers every 2-3 months to ensure freshness and safety.

  • Provide Single-Use Tools

    Always have disposable applicators, cotton swabs, and spatulas available.

Store Management Guidelines

  • Display Hygiene Guidelines

    Post clear instructions for both staff and customers in visible locations.

  • Dispose Expired Products

    Regularly check and discard any expired or contaminated cosmetics.

  • Provide Hand Sanitizers

    Make alcohol-based hand sanitizers readily available throughout the store.
